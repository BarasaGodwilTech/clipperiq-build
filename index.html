How it works
  1. Upload — the video is sent to your ClipperIQ server in small pieces, so big files and weak connections are fine.
  2. Analyse — audio peaks and scene changes are scored to find the strongest moments (or the whole video is split into parts in Series mode).
  3. Cut — FFmpeg on the server renders each clip with your shape, overlays and music.
  4. Review — clips appear in Generated Clips, ready to download or schedule.
